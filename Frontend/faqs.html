Frequently Asked Questions

Yes — every painting on Kalakar is an original, hand-painted piece made by one of our partner artists. We don't sell prints of paintings unless a listing specifically says 'giclée print' or 'art print.' Each original ships with a signed certificate of authenticity.
Domestic orders arrive within 3–7 business days depending on location. International orders take 7–14 business days. Made-to-order and custom pieces may add 2–4 weeks to that timeline — this is always noted on the product page.
Yes, most ready-to-ship paintings can be returned within 15 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Custom commissions and made-to-order pieces are final sale unless damaged in transit — see our Returns & Exchanges page for full details.
Absolutely. Many of our artists accept commissions — send us a reference photo, preferred size and medium via our Contact page, and we'll connect you with an artist and provide a quote within 3–5 business days.
All our woollen pieces are hand-woven or hand-knitted using wool and Pashmina sourced from ethically-reared yaks, sheep and goats in Himalayan communities. No power looms are used — every piece takes days to weeks to complete.
Sizes vary by piece and are listed on each product page, typically ranging from 10×14 in to 30×40 in. Need a custom size? Reach out via our Contact page and we'll check with the artist.
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout. Any import duties or local taxes charged by your country are the responsibility of the recipient.
Paintings should be kept away from direct sunlight and humidity, and dusted gently with a dry, soft cloth. Woollens should be hand-washed in cold water with a mild detergent or dry-cleaned, and stored folded rather than hung.
Yes — you'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS once your order ships. You can also check status anytime on our Order Tracking page using your order number.
Yes, we work with businesses on curated corporate gifting — from festive hampers to branded packaging for bulk orders. Visit our Corporate Gifting page or contact us to discuss your requirements.
