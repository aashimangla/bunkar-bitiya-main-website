Meet the artisans, painters and weavers of Kalakar

Since 2014

Art Born of the Mountains

Kalakar began as a small collective of painters and weavers working out of two studios in the foothills of the Himalayas. Today we work with over 60 artisan partners across Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Kashmir — bringing their paintings, shawls and handcrafted pieces to homes across India and the world, without ever losing the hand that made them.

How We Began

Two Studios,
One Shared Craft

Our founder grew up watching her grandmother dye wool by hand in a small village near Manali. Kalakar was started to give that same craft — and the artisans who keep it alive — a wider audience, without asking them to compromise on tradition or trade away fair wages for scale.

The Painters

Light Painted
from Memory

Every painting in our collection is created by an artist working from their own studio — many of them overlooking the very peaks, valleys and monasteries they paint. Nothing is printed or mass-produced; each canvas carries the brushwork, the pressure, and the small imperfections of a human hand.
