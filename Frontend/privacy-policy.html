Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

Last updated: January 1, 2026

At Kalakar, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, phone number, shipping address and payment details when you place an order, create an account, or contact us. We also collect limited technical data (browser type, device, pages visited) to improve our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process and deliver orders, communicate order updates, respond to enquiries, send marketing emails (only if you've opted in), and improve our products and website experience.

3. Payment Information

All payments are processed through Razorpay and other PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. Kalakar does not store your full card details on our servers.

4. Sharing of Information

We share your information only with trusted service providers — such as courier partners for delivery and payment gateways for transactions — strictly to fulfil your order. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your cart, preferences, and to understand site usage through analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

6. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing hello@kalakar.in. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any newsletter.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including SSL encryption, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@kalakar.in or visit our Contact page.
