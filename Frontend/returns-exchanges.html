Returns & Exchanges

Our Return Policy

We want you to love every piece you bring home. If something isn't right, most items can be returned or exchanged within 15 days of delivery, provided they're unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging.

Eligible for Return

Woollen wear and handcrafted gifts in unused, original condition with tags attached. Paintings that arrive damaged or significantly different from their listing.

Not Eligible for Return

Custom commissions, made-to-order paintings, personalised gifts, and sale items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned unless damaged in transit.

How to Start a Return

Email hello@kalakar.in with your order number and reason for return. We'll send a prepaid return label for domestic orders and arrange pickup within 2–3 business days.

Refund Timeline

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method. You'll get an email confirmation once it's done.
