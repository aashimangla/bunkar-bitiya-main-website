Shipping Information

How We Ship Your Order

Domestic Shipping (India)

All domestic orders above ₹999 ship free via our trusted courier partners. Orders below ₹999 incur a flat ₹99 shipping fee. Metro cities typically receive orders within 3–5 business days; other locations within 5–7 business days.

International Shipping

We ship to over 40 countries. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination, and delivery takes 7–14 business days. Import duties and taxes, if applicable, are the responsibility of the recipient.

Packaging

Paintings are shipped in rigid, corner-protected boxes with acid-free tissue. Woollens and gifts arrive in our signature Kalakar gift box with a personalised message card, wrapped in recycled kraft paper.

Order Processing Time

Most orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days. Made-to-order and custom commission pieces may take 2–4 weeks — the exact timeline is shown on the product page.

Tracking Your Order

You'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as your order ships. You can also track any order anytime on our Order Tracking page.
